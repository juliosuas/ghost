# Acceptable use

Ghost is for authorized investigations and self-audits only.

You may run Ghost only when you are allowed to investigate the target. A self-audit of accounts you control is the expected personal use. Examples, issues, and tests stay on synthetic data such as `demo_user`.

## Not allowed

- Stalking
- Harassment
- Doxxing
- Targeting people without authorization

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for complying with the law that applies to you. That includes privacy and computer-access rules. The maintainers do not authorize you to investigate any person.

If you are not sure the investigation is authorized, do not run Ghost against that target.

See also the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
